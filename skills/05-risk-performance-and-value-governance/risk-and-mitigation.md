# Risk and Mitigation

## When To Use

Use this skill when a strategy or initiative needs a serious risk assessment before approval. It fits board materials, investment cases, product launches, transformations, and major operational changes.

## Strategy-Consulting Approach

Treat risk as part of strategy, not a compliance exercise. Identify risks that could change the decision, the economics, the timing, or the viability.

## Workflow

1. Define the strategy or initiative being assessed.
2. Identify risks across market, customer, competitor, financial, operational, regulatory, and organizational dimensions.
3. Score likelihood and impact.
4. Design mitigations, early warnings, owners, and contingency actions.
5. Assess residual risk after mitigations.
6. Recommend proceed, gate, redesign, or stop.

## Output Format

```markdown
# Risk and Mitigation

## Strategy Under Review
[Brief description.]

## Risk Register
| Risk | Category | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner | Early Warning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Critical Assumptions
[Assumptions that must hold for the strategy to work.]

## Contingency Actions
[What to do if key risks materialize.]
```

## Quality Bar

- Focus on risks that could change the decision.
- Assign owners and early warning indicators.
- Show residual risk after mitigation.
- Use specific language, not boilerplate.
