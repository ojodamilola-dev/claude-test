# Business Case Builder

## When To Use

Use this skill when a strategic choice needs quantified economics and a clear investment decision. It fits new products, market expansion, transformation programs, pricing changes, M&A, and major funding requests.

## Strategy-Consulting Approach

Build economics from assumptions, not spreadsheets alone. Show base, downside, and upside cases. Make assumptions visible and testable.

## Workflow

1. Define the decision and investment option.
2. Identify value drivers, cost drivers, timing, and risks.
3. Build base, downside, and upside cases.
4. Surface load-bearing assumptions.
5. Calculate relevant metrics: ROI, payback, NPV, margin impact, or cash impact.
6. Recommend proceed, test, redesign, or stop.

## Output Format

```markdown
# Business Case

## Decision
[What is being decided.]

## Economics Summary
| Case | Revenue Impact | Cost | Net Value | Payback |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Value Drivers
| Driver | Assumption | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|

## Cost Structure
| Category | Amount | Timing |
|---|---|---|

## Risks
| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|

## Recommendation
[Proceed, test, redesign, or stop with rationale.]
```

## Quality Bar

- Show assumptions and confidence, not only numbers.
- Include downside and sensitivity, not only the base case.
- Match metrics to the actual decision.
- Avoid false precision in long-range projections.
