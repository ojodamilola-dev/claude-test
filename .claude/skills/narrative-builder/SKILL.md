# Narrative Builder

## When To Use

Use this skill when the recommendation is mostly known but the story needs to land quickly with executives, boards, investors, or leadership teams.

## Strategy-Consulting Approach

Combine Pyramid Principle structure, SCQA framing, answer-first communication, and hostile-question prep to build narratives that persuade and withstand scrutiny.

## Workflow

1. Clarify audience, desired action, and constraints.
2. Extract the core recommendation.
3. Choose the story pattern: answer-first, SCQA, or situation-response.
4. Build the headline sequence.
5. Prep hostile questions and answers.
6. Produce the narrative outline.

## Output Format

```markdown
# Executive Narrative

## Audience and Action
[Who, what they must decide or do.]

## Core Message
[One sentence.]

## Storyline
| Slide or Section | Headline | Supporting Points |
|---|---|---|

## Pyramid Logic
| Level | Content |
|---|---|

## Hostile Questions
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
```

## Quality Bar

- The first minute should reveal the recommendation.
- Headlines should tell the story without body text.
- Remove setup language that delays the answer.
- Prep answers to the three hardest questions.
